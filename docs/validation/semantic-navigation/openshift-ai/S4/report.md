## Attempt-3 armed-failure diagnosis (October 31, continued session)

The trial-10 failure was re-examined against the retained wire and runtime evidence; the earlier "bursty delivery" reading is corrected. `motion_verified` was not missed by the host runner — the fixture never reached it. The failure is a structural lease-renewal race in the fixture's semantic-evidence plumbing, and the serving path was healthy throughout: the proxy returned 200 for every request past the stop, the adapter emitted 77 results with a final 191 ms capture-age bound, and the injection log is empty.

The gate's semantic budget runs 500 ms from the **capture time** of the last accepted result (the unchanged capture-age authority). A renewal travels capture → adapter result publish (~250–270 ms in the armed window: pipeline plus a 133–152 ms backend) → observer-worker receipt (~30 ms) → worker status write (10 ms cadence) → fixture `semantic_lease_poll` (20 ms granularity) → `gate.observe`. Results are emitted every ~182–206 ms of wall time — every other 15 Hz frame at RTF ≈ 0.69 with one HTTP request in flight — so each renewal lands ~290–320 ms after its capture while the previous capture's deadline expires 500 ms after that capture. Measured against the previous deadline, the next result's **observer-side** margin was median ≈ 85–91 ms and p10 ≈ 52–70 ms across all ten trials of attempt 3, passing trials included; the final ~30 ms of plumbing to the gate comes out of that.

Trial 10 lost this race by milliseconds. The result captured at wall 1793480185.558 (stamp 10.956 s) reached the observer at 1793480185.839 — 18.4 ms before the previous capture's deadline (1793480185.857) — but the status write plus the 20 ms poll granularity delivered it to the gate only at ~1793480185.85–.87, after the guard's revoke tick at 1793480185.855. The guard then correctly failed closed with `semantic_stale`. One pre-arm observer-side miss of −14.3 ms (wall ≈ 1793480178.58) was harmless because the gate was not yet armed. With a healthy backend, all passing trials of the attempt ran on the same thin margins; this trial is the realized tail of that race, not an inference outage.

Consequence for the continuation plan: shortening the host runner's 500 ms fixture-status poll in `run_g4_faults.py` would not have prevented and cannot prevent this failure class — the host poll acts only after `motion_verified`, which was never reached. The candidate levers that do not touch any acceptance criterion (500 ms freshness, 100 ms zero-command, fault semantics, identity) are the lease-evidence transport latency (worker status-write cadence and fixture poll granularity, worth ~25–30 ms), the adapter's capture-to-publish latency, and the candidate camera cadence. Any such change requires a fresh fixture image, recorded source hashes and rationale, and a full re-qualification; none has been made.
