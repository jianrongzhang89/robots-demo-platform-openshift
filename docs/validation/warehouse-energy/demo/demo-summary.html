5. Resilience: serving endpoint unavailable

Before a new route decision the energy endpoint was made unreachable. The planner activated its documented fallback to the local calibrated physics model with the conservative uncertainty allowance, selected route A, and completed the mission (0.4402 Wh consumed, 157 s, estimator mode fallback). No mission starts on an unvalidated prediction.
Scope. Simulation demonstration over modeled energy. The consumed values are outputs of the frozen telemetry model, not physical battery measurements; no energy saving is claimed. Route A is both the shortest and the energy-optimal corridor in this fixture, so the three arms select the same route by design of the physics, not by failure.
